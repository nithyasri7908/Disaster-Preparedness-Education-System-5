HackVerse 2.0

Official Guidelines

National-Level Project Showcasing Hackathon

School of Computer Science and Engineering

Event Overview

HackVerse 2.0 is a National-Level Project Showcasing Hackathon organized by School of Computer Science and Engineering.

Key Features:

  • Platform for innovative, practical, and impactful projects
  • Diverse domains of participation
  • Demonstration-focused event
  • Innovation and presentation driven
  • Not a time-bound development sprint
Focus: Demonstration, Innovation, and Presentation

Eligibility Criteria

  • Open to: Students enrolled in recognized colleges/universities
  • Participation: Individual or teams of up to 4 members
  • Mandatory: Valid student ID is required

Team Lead Requirements:

  • All team leaders will be provided with a team lead T-Shirt
  • It is COMPULSORY to wear the T-Shirt during the Hackathon

Project Guidelines

Project Requirements:

  • Projects must be functional (prototype/MVP)
  • Projects must be original
  • Previously developed projects are allowed if owned by participants

Prohibited:

  • Purely theoretical ideas
  • Plagiarized or copied projects
Important: All team leaders must wear their provided T-Shirts compulsorily during the hackathon.

Domains of Participation

  • AI/ML - Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  • Web & Mobile Development - Web and app-based solutions
  • Cybersecurity - Security and privacy solutions
  • IoT - Internet of Things and embedded systems
  • Blockchain - Distributed ledger technologies
  • Open Innovation - Any other innovative domain
Note: Open Innovation evaluation is based on merit and impact.

Presentation Format

Time Allocation:

  • Will be decided by the judging panel

Presentation Must Include:

  • Problem Statement - What problem does your project solve?
  • Solution - Your approach to solving it
  • Implementation - Technical details and architecture
  • Demo - Live demonstration of the project
  • Impact - Real-world benefits and outcomes

Q&A Session:

  • Will follow the presentation
6 / 18

Evaluation Criteria

Projects will be evaluated on:

  • Innovation - Novelty and creativity of the solution
  • Technical Complexity - Depth of technical implementation
  • Practical Impact - Real-world applicability and benefits
  • Presentation Skills - Clarity and communication
  • Prototype Functionality - Working and bug-free implementation
Tip: Focus on delivering a working prototype with clear presentation and real-world impact.

Anonymity Policy

To ensure fair evaluation:

  • Participants must NOT disclose their university/college name
  • Avoid displaying institution name at ANY stage

What NOT to do:

  • Include in presentation slides
  • Mention in verbal introductions
  • Display in project demonstrations

Official Communication:

  • Use Team Name or Team ID for all interactions
⚠️ Warning: Violation of this policy may result in disqualification.
